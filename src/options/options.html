Error reports

Error reports

A report says which step failed and the kind of error, the web address that failed with its IDs removed, the field Real Signature refused and the rule it broke, choices like "deal matched by address" or "2–3 forms", how many drafts this browser has made and how many failed, and the Dotted Line and Chrome versions. A report never contains your forms, or any name, email, address, deal or template. Your IP address isn't kept, and reports are deleted after 12 months. Everything a report can hold.
