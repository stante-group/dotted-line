Dotted Line

Help us fix problems faster?

Dotted Line can send the Deal Dates team a short report when a draft fails, and once a day a count of drafts that worked.

What's in a report

Which step failed and the kind of error; the web address that failed with its IDs removed; the name of the field Real Signature refused and the rule it broke (like "email subject: at most 70 characters"); choices like "deal matched by address", "2–3 forms" or "acting as an agent"; the form type's code (like AREA0287) when blocks couldn't be placed from a form's labels; how many drafts this browser has made and how many failed; and the Dotted Line and Chrome versions.

A report never contains your forms, or any name, email, address, deal or template. (Your forms still go from WEBForms to Real Signature to make the draft, as always.) Your IP address isn't kept. Reports are deleted after 12 months. Everything a report can hold.

You can change this in Settings. With No, an error still offers to send that one report, only if you press it.

Turn forms into a Real Signature draft

  1. In WEBForms, tick the forms on a deal, or open one form.
  2. Click the Dotted Line icon. You check the draft in Real Signature and send it yourself.
