Dotted Line

Help us fix problems faster?

Dotted Line can send the Deal Dates team a short report when a draft fails, and once a day a count of drafts that worked.

What's in a report

Which step failed and the kind of error; the web address that failed with its IDs removed; the name of the field Real Signature refused and the rule it broke (like "email subject: at most 70 characters"); choices like "deal matched by address", "2–3 forms" or "acting as an agent"; how many drafts this browser has made and how many failed; and the Dotted Line and Chrome versions.

A report never contains your forms, or any name, email, address, deal or template. (Your forms still go from WEBForms to Real Signature to make the draft, as always.) Your IP address isn't kept. Reports are deleted after 12 months. Everything a report can hold.

You can change this in Settings. With No, an error still offers to send that one report, only if you press it.

    Templates

    A template places the signature, initial and date blocks for you. Only your Real Signature templates that have blocks and the same number of pages as the form are listed. Your choice is remembered for next time. With no template, you place the blocks in Real Signature.

    Signers

    As many as are named on the form; two when the form names nobody on that side. Blocks for signers beyond these numbers are left off.
